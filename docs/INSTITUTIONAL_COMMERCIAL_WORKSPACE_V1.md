# Institutional Commercial Workspace v1

**Status:** implementation candidate  
**Base:** `main@a478c4d59973e1453546515c7099b1e813dd1502`

## Purpose

Provide one executive/commercial entry point across the institutional planning surfaces already proven in Promomed.

The workspace connects:

1. Buyer Fit Matrix;
2. Pilot Proposal Studio;
3. Institutional Outreach Pack;
4. Diligence Command Center.

## Guided path

### 1. Buyer Fit
Question:

Which institutional archetypes have a direct capability match, and which assumptions still require real buyer validation?

Boundary:

`capability fit != market demand`.

### 2. Proposal Studio
Question:

What bounded pilot shape can be proposed for the archetype, and what remains TO AGREE?

Boundary:

`proposal != commitment`.

### 3. Outreach Pack
Question:

How should the first serious buyer conversation be structured?

Boundary:

`meeting brief != meeting occurred`.

### 4. Command Center
Question:

Once attributable institutional diligence exists, what blocks pilot/procurement/legal/security and who owns the next action?

Boundary:

`READY FOR DECISION != APPROVED`.

## Print / PDF

The workspace includes print CSS and a user-triggered print/PDF action.

The printed document remains a planning guide.

It does not become:

- a commercial offer;
- a contract;
- a meeting record;
- an approval;
- a pipeline report;
- evidence of customer intent.

## Technical boundary

This layer adds:

- no API;
- no table;
- no migration;
- no CRM opportunity;
- no pipeline stage;
- no scoring;
- no mutable authority.

It is a static navigation/presentation layer over existing proven surfaces.

## Truth boundary

- workspace != pipeline;
- navigation != buyer intent;
- READY FOR DECISION != APPROVED;
- price / PO / revenue not claimed;
- External Participation Acceptance remains GATED.

## Commercial consequence

Promomed now has a coherent institutional commercial operating route rather than a collection of disconnected enterprise screens.

A seller, CEO or strategic partner can understand which surface to use before, during and after a real institutional conversation without mixing preparation, diligence and approval.
