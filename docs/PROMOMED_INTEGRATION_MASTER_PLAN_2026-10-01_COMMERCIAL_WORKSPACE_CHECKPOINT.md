## Institutional Commercial Workspace v1

Purpose:

unify the already-proven institutional commercial/readiness surfaces into one guided route without adding any new customer/pipeline authority.

Path:

`Buyer Fit -> Proposal Studio -> Outreach Pack -> Command Center`.

Hard boundaries:

- no new API;
- no new table or migration;
- no lead/opportunity/pipeline persistence;
- no customer intent claim;
- no win probability or pipeline value;
- no approval semantics;
- print/PDF remains planning material;
- External Participation Acceptance remains `GATED`.

Commercial consequence:

the institutional enterprise stack becomes easier to demonstrate and operate because every stage answers one distinct buyer/commercial question.
