## Institutional Buyer Fit Matrix v1

Purpose:

compare institutional partner archetypes by Promomed capability match while preserving the difference between planning and real market evidence.

Flow:

`archetype -> capability dimensions -> strong/conditional match -> validation questions -> proposal reference -> planning export`.

Hard boundaries:

- no synthetic numeric score;
- no market-demand claim;
- no win-probability claim;
- no revenue-potential claim;
- no customer-priority claim;
- no real pipeline claim;
- no real pilot claim;
- External Participation Acceptance remains `GATED`.

Commercial consequence:

sales/product leadership can prepare which value story, stakeholders and diligence questions matter for each institutional archetype without turning product fit into fabricated traction.

## Next dependency after green merge/live proof

Use Proposal Studio + Buyer Fit Matrix to prepare real outreach.

If no real buyer is yet available, the next safe product layer is **Institutional Outreach Pack / First-Meeting Brief**:

- archetype-specific meeting agenda;
- discovery questions;
- evidence to show;
- claims that must not be made;
- follow-up outputs expected from the meeting.

That layer must remain planning-only and must not create CRM pipeline truth or customer intent.
